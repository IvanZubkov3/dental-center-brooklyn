What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive dental care — from routine cleanings to advanced implant surgery — all under one roof in Brooklyn.

Preventive Care Cosmetic Orthodontics Restorative & Surgical
Preventive Care

Keep your smile healthy

Exams & Cleanings

Routine exams, professional cleanings, digital X-rays, and personalized oral health recommendations to keep you ahead of any issues.

Fluoride Treatments

Professional fluoride application to strengthen enamel, reduce sensitivity, and protect against cavity formation — especially for children and high-risk patients.

Emergency Dentistry

Same-day appointments for tooth pain, swelling, broken or knocked-out teeth. Call us — we prioritize urgent cases and will fit you in quickly.

Laser Pocket Disinfection (LPD)

A non-surgical laser dental procedure used to treat gingivitis and periodontitis (gum disease).

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your best smile

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile design, bonding, and aesthetic corrections tailored to your facial features and goals. Look and feel confident every day.

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin, custom-crafted porcelain shells that transform the shape, color, and alignment of your teeth for a natural, luminous result.

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-office whitening for dramatic results, plus custom take-home trays for ongoing touch-ups — both safe and effective.

Orthodontics

Straighter, aligned teeth

Invisalign

Clear, removable aligners for a straighter smile — discreet and comfortable. We'll assess your fit and walk you through the full treatment timeline.

Orthodontics

Comprehensive bite and alignment evaluation with guidance on the best orthodontic approach for your needs and lifestyle.

Gum Health — Periodontics

Deep cleaning, scaling and root planing, and ongoing periodontal maintenance by our Harvard-trained board-certified periodontist.

Restorative & Surgical

Repair & replace

Restorative Dentistry

Fillings, crowns, bridges, and full-mouth rehabilitation — explained clearly, with options that fit your budget and goals.

Root Canals

Comfortable, relief-focused care for infected or abscessed teeth. We use modern techniques to make the experience as easy as possible.

Oral Surgery & Sedation

Surgical procedures with sedation options for maximum comfort and minimal recovery time. We'll guide you through every step.

Extractions — Wisdom Teeth

Simple and surgical extractions performed with care. We provide detailed aftercare guidance to ensure smooth, comfortable healing.

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement. Our board-certified periodontist specializes in single-tooth and full-arch implant solutions.

CBCT 3D Imaging

Advanced cone beam CT imaging for precise diagnosis and implant planning — a higher standard of care for complex treatment cases.
