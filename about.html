About Us

A family-led practice dedicated to modern dentistry, patient comfort, and lasting relationships with the Brooklyn community.

Modern dentistry, with a comfort-first philosophy.

Established in 1980, the Dental Center of Brooklyn has proudly served the community for over four decades. What began as a small neighborhood office has grown into a modern, multidisciplinary practice offering comprehensive care for patients of all ages.

Throughout our history, we've remained committed to excellence, innovation, and patient-centered care — adopting advanced technologies and techniques to achieve the best possible outcomes. Generations of families have trusted us with their smiles.
